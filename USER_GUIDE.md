# MMM Booking App — User Guide

## For Clients

### Creating an Account
1. Open the app, tap **"New here? Register."**
2. Fill in your full name, email, phone number, and a password.
3. Read and tick the consent checkbox (you can tap "About," "Privacy Policy," or "Terms and Conditions" to read each one first).
4. Tap **Submit**.
5. Check your email and click the verification link.
6. Return to the app and log in.

### Booking an Appointment
1. From Home, tap **Book Appointment**.
2. Choose a service, then choose Online or In-Person.
   - If In-Person, also choose a location (Midrand, Johannesburg, or Centurion).
3. Choose a duration, date, and time. The app will not let you pick a weekend, a time outside 08:00–18:00, or a South African public holiday.
4. Add any notes if you'd like, then tap **Submit Booking Request**.
5. Your practice will contact you with a fee quotation before confirming.

### Managing Your Bookings
- Go to **My Bookings** to see all your appointments and their status (Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled, Completed).
- To cancel, tap **Cancel** on a booking and confirm — **this cannot be undone.**

### Settings
- Toggle **Dark Mode** and **Booking Reminders** from Settings.
- Change your password (you'll need to enter your current password first).
- To delete your account, tap **Delete My Account** — this opens an email to the practice, who will confirm once it's done.

### Logging Out
You can log out from Home (button beside your name) or from Settings (button at the top).

## For Admin (Practice Staff)

### Viewing Bookings
- The Admin Dashboard shows all client bookings, a visual chart of booking status, and filter/search tools.
- Use the **search box** to find a booking by service, location, or notes.
- Use the **Status**, **Location**, and **Date** filters to narrow the list — search and filters work together.

### Managing a Booking
Tap any booking to open its details, then Confirm, Decline, Mark as Paid, or Mark as Completed.

### Viewing Analytics
Tap **View Detailed Analytics** for a breakdown of bookings and registrations by day/week/month/year, with a simple week-over-week trend estimate.

### Exporting Data
Tap **Export to CSV** to save or share the currently-filtered list of bookings as a spreadsheet file.

### Updating Practice Information
From Settings → Practice Information, update your contact number, email, WhatsApp link, and operating hours — changes apply across the app immediately.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Can't log in | Email not verified yet | Check your inbox for the verification email |
| "You're offline" message | No internet connection | Booking, login, and cancellation need a connection; other screens work from your last-loaded data |
| Booking rejected on a weekday | Date is a South African public holiday | Choose a different date |
| Can't find Logout | — | Look at the top of Home, top of Settings, or the Admin Dashboard header |
| Forgot password | — | Tap "Forgot Password?" on the Login screen |
