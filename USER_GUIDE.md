## For Clients

### Creating an Account

1. Open the app and tap **"New here? Register"**.
2. Fill in your full name, email, **confirm your email by typing it again**, phone number, and password.
3. Read and tick the consent checkbox — tap **"About,"** **"Privacy Policy,"** or **"Terms and Conditions"** within the sentence to read each one first.
4. _(Optional)_ Tick **"Subscribe to news and updates"** if you'd like to hear from the practice.
5. Tap **Submit**. Any problem is shown next to the field it belongs to — for example, the password rules turn **red** if your password doesn't meet them.
6. Check your email and click the verification link.
7. Return to the app and log in.

### Logging In

1. Enter your email and password, then tap **Log In**. If you leave a field empty, a message appears on that field.
2. A brief **"Logged in successfully"** confirmation appears as your Home screen loads.
3. **You'll stay logged in** even after fully closing the app — you only need to log in again if you tap **Logout** yourself.

### The Home Screen

Your Home screen shows:

- Your name and a Logout icon _(top)_
- The practice's branding card
- A **Next Appointment** card with the main **Book Appointment** button
- Four cards: **Services**, **My Bookings**, **Contact**, **Settings**
- A support bar at the bottom for quick access to Contact

> **A note on the back button:** Home and the Admin Dashboard are your app's "home base" screens. Pressing your phone's back button once on either screen will **not** log you out or close the app — you'll see a brief **"Press back again to exit"** message instead. Pressing back a second time within ~2 seconds closes the app _(you stay logged in)_.

### Booking an Appointment

1. From Home, tap **Book Appointment**.
2. Choose a service, then **Online** or **In-Person** _(if In-Person, also choose **Midrand**, **Johannesburg** or **Centurion** - addresses are listed under [Practice Locations](#practice-locations))_.
3. Choose a **duration**, **date** and **start time**. The app checks, and tells you why if something isn't allowed:
   - The practice must be **open that day** (open days are set by the practice and shown on the Contact screen).
   - The date must not be a **South African public holiday**.
   - Start times are on the **quarter hour** (:00, :15, :30 or :45).
   - The **whole appointment must finish before closing time** — for example, a 60-minute appointment can't start at 17:45 if the practice closes at 18:00. If you lengthen the duration and your chosen time no longer fits, you'll be asked to choose the time again.
   - The time must be **free** — if another client (or you) already has an appointment that overlaps it at that location, you'll be asked to choose a different time.
4. Add any notes if you'd like, then tap **Submit Booking Request**.
5. The practice will contact you with a fee quotation before confirming.
6. If **Booking Reminders** is on (it is by default), you'll receive an immediate confirmation notification, plus a reminder the evening before your appointment.

### Managing Your Bookings

- **My Bookings** shows all your appointments and their status.
- **Status updates automatically** — if the practice confirms or declines your booking while you have this screen open, it updates in real time with no need to refresh.
- To cancel a pending or confirmed booking, tap **Cancel** and confirm. The time slot is released immediately so someone else can book it. Cancelling cannot be undone — to change your appointment time, cancel it and make a new booking.
- Cancelled bookings stay in your list as a record. Only the practice can permanently remove a booking record.

### Settings

| Setting | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Name and phone** | Edit and tap **Save Changes** |
| **Booking Reminders** | Turns **all** booking notifications on or off (the instant confirmation and the evening-before reminder). Switching it off also cancels reminders you already have scheduled; switching it back on schedules them again. A confirmation message appears each time. |
| **Dark Mode** | Switches the whole app's look; a confirmation message appears |
| **Change Password** | Requires your current password first, as a security check |
| **Change Email Address** | Requires your current password. A confirmation link is sent to your **new** address, and your login email only changes once you click it. |
| **Delete My Account** | Explains the process first. **Continue** opens your email app with the deletion request pre-addressed to the practice (or **Cancel** to back out). The practice removes your account and data and confirms by email. |

### Logging Out

Available from **Home** _(icon beside your name)_ or **Settings** _(bottom of the Support section)_. Either way, you'll be asked to confirm — tap **Log Out** to proceed, or **Cancel** to stay logged in. Once confirmed, you'll need to log in again.

## For Admin (Practice Staff)

### Viewing Bookings

- The **Admin Dashboard** shows all client bookings, a visual overview card, search, and filters.
- **The booking list updates automatically** — a new client booking, or a change made from the Booking Detail screen, appears in real time with no need to refresh.
- Use the search box to find a booking by service, location, or notes.
- Search and filters _(Status / Location / Date)_ work together.

### Managing a Booking

Tap any booking to open its details, then choose:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirm** | Marks a pending booking as confirmed |
| **Decline** | Asks you to confirm, then marks the booking **cancelled** and frees its time slot. The record is **kept** for history. |
| **Mark as Completed** | Marks a confirmed booking as completed |
| **Paid switch** | Records whether the client has paid |
| **Delete Booking** | Asks you to confirm, then **permanently removes** the booking record. It cannot be undone. Use **Decline** instead if you want to keep a record of a cancelled appointment. |

### Viewing Analytics

Tap **"View Detailed Analytics"** for a breakdown of bookings and registrations by day, week, month, or year, including:

- Percentage change over each period
- A visual bar-chart comparison across periods
- A simple trend estimate for the coming week _(clearly labelled as a straightforward calculation, not AI)_

### Exporting Data

Tap **"Export CSV"** to save or share the currently-filtered list of bookings as a spreadsheet file.

### Updating Practice Information

From **Settings → Practice Information**:

- Update the contact number, email, WhatsApp link and crisis-line note.
- **Open Days:** tick the days the practice is open. Clients can only book on ticked days, and the Contact screen shows them as readable text (for example "Monday - Friday"). At least one day must be ticked.
- **Opening and closing time:** enter them as `HH:mm` (for example `08:00` and `18:00`). Opening must be earlier than closing. Clients can only book appointments that **finish** by the closing time.

Changes apply across the app immediately.

### Logging Out

Tap the Logout icon in the **Admin Dashboard** header, or from **Settings**. Either way, you'll be asked to confirm before being signed out.

## Using the App on a Tablet

The app is fully usable on both phones and tablets:

- On a larger tablet screen, content is **centred with generous margins** rather than stretched edge-to-edge.
- Text is **sized larger** for comfortable reading at arm's length.
- Works in portrait and landscape; screens scroll when the content is taller than the display.
- All the same features and screens work identically — nothing is hidden or different on tablet.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely Cause | What to Do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Can't log in | Email not verified yet | Check your inbox for the verification email; the Login screen offers to resend it |
| "You're offline" message | No internet connection | Booking, login, and cancellation need a connection; other screens work from your last-loaded data |
| "The practice is closed on that day" | You picked a day the practice isn't open | Choose one of the open days shown in the message |
| Booking rejected on a weekday | Date is a South African public holiday | Choose a different date |
| "Appointments start on the quarter hour" | You picked a start time like 10:20 | Choose :00, :15, :30 or :45 |
| "…would finish after closing time" | The appointment would run past closing | Choose an earlier start time or a shorter duration |
| "Already booked by another client" / "You already have a booking that overlaps" | That time is taken at that location | Choose a different time, or a different location |
| "Registration failed" | The account could not be fully created (for example a connection problem) | Nothing was created — check your connection and try again |
| Booking confirmation or reminder notification didn't appear | Booking Reminders is switched off, or notifications are blocked for the app | Check Settings → Booking Reminders, and the phone's notification permission for the app |
| Can't find Logout | — | Home _(top-right icon)_ or Settings _(bottom of Support section)_ — either way you'll be asked to confirm |
| Forgot password | — | Tap **"Forgot Password?"** on Login |
| Pressed back and nothing happened | You're on Home or Admin Dashboard | Expected — press back again within 2 seconds to exit the app |
| Got logged out unexpectedly | This shouldn't happen | You are only ever logged out by tapping Logout and confirming — if it happens any other way, report it to the practice as a bug |
| Can't cancel an old booking | Very old test bookings made before the security update | Contact the practice; an admin can decline or remove it |
| I think my email has been compromised or I've lost access to it | — | While you're still logged in and still know your password, go to Settings → Change Email Address immediately to move your account to a safe email |
| I've lost BOTH my password and access to my email | This can't be recovered automatically — no email/password app anywhere can do this safely | Contact the practice directly by phone or WhatsApp (shown on the Contact screen even when logged out) — staff can assist you manually |
