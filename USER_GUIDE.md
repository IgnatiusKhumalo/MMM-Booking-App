# USER_GUIDE.md

## For Clients

### Creating an Account

1. Open the app, tap "New here? Register"
2. Fill in full name, email, phone number, password
3. Read and tick the consent checkbox (tap "About," "Privacy Policy," or "Terms and Conditions" within the sentence to read each one first)
4. Tap Submit
5. Check your email, click the verification link
6. Return to the app and log in

### Booking an Appointment

1. From Home, tap Book Appointment
2. Choose a service, then Online or In-Person (if In-Person, also choose a location)
3. Choose duration, date, and time — the app won't allow weekends, times outside 08:00–18:00, or South African public holidays
4. Add any notes if you'd like, tap Submit Booking Request
5. The practice will contact you with a fee quotation before confirming

### Managing Your Bookings

- My Bookings shows all your appointments and their status
- To cancel, tap Cancel and confirm — this cannot be undone

### Settings

- Toggle Dark Mode and Booking Reminders
- Change your password (current password required first)
- Delete My Account opens an email to the practice, who will confirm once done

### Logging Out

Available from Home (icon beside your name) or Settings (bottom of the Support section).

## For Admin (Practice Staff)

### Viewing Bookings

- Admin Dashboard shows all client bookings, a visual overview card, search, and filters
- Use the search box to find a booking by service, location, or notes
- Use the Status/Location/Date filter pills — search and filters work together

### Managing a Booking

Tap any booking to open its details, then Confirm, Decline, Mark as Paid, or Mark as Completed.

### Viewing Analytics

Tap "View Detailed Analytics" for a breakdown of bookings and registrations by day/week/month/year, with a simple trend estimate for the coming week.

### Exporting Data

Tap "Export CSV" to save or share the currently-filtered list of bookings as a spreadsheet file.

### Updating Practice Information

From Settings → Practice Information, update contact number, email, WhatsApp link, and operating hours — changes apply across the app immediately.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Can't log in | Email not verified yet | Check your inbox for the verification email |
| "You're offline" message | No internet connection | Booking, login, and cancellation need a connection; other screens work from your last-loaded data |
| Booking rejected on a weekday | Date is a South African public holiday | Choose a different date |
| Can't find Logout | — | Home (top-right icon) or Settings (bottom of Support section) |
| Forgot password | — | Tap "Forgot Password?" on Login |
