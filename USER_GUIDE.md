## For Clients

### Creating an Account

1. Open the app and tap **"New here? Register"**.
2. Fill in your full name, email, phone number, and password.
3. Read and tick the consent checkbox — tap **"About,"** **"Privacy Policy,"** or **"Terms and Conditions"** within the sentence to read each one first.
4. *(Optional)* Tick **"Subscribe to news and updates"** if you'd like to hear from the practice.
5. Tap **Submit**.
6. Check your email and click the verification link.
7. Return to the app and log in.

### Logging In

1. Enter your email and password, then tap **Log In**.
2. A brief **"Logged in successfully"** confirmation appears as your Home screen loads.
3. **You'll stay logged in** even after fully closing the app — you only need to log in again if you tap **Logout** yourself. There's no need to log in every time you reopen the app.

### The Home Screen

Your Home screen shows:

- Your name and a Logout icon *(top)*
- The practice's branding card
- A **Next Appointment** card with the main **Book Appointment** button
- Four cards: **Services**, **My Bookings**, **Contact**, **Settings**
- A support bar at the bottom for quick access to Contact

> **A note on the back button:** Home and the Admin Dashboard are your app's "home base" screens. Pressing your phone's back button once on either screen will **not** log you out or close the app — you'll see a brief **"Press back again to exit"** message instead. Pressing back a second time within ~2 seconds closes the app *(you stay logged in)*. This is by design, so you can't accidentally exit or get logged out with one stray tap.

### Booking an Appointment

1. From Home, tap **Book Appointment**.
2. Choose a service, then **Online** or **In-Person** *(if In-Person, also choose a location)*.
3. Choose a duration, date, and time — the app won't allow weekends, times outside **08:00–18:00**, or South African public holidays.
4. Add any notes if you'd like, then tap **Submit Booking Request**.
5. The practice will contact you with a fee quotation before confirming.
6. You'll receive an immediate confirmation notification, plus a reminder notification the evening before your appointment.

### Managing Your Bookings

- **My Bookings** shows all your appointments and their status.
- **Status updates automatically** — if the practice confirms or declines your booking while you have this screen open, it updates in real time with no need to refresh or reopen.
- To cancel, tap **Cancel** and confirm — this action cannot be undone.

### Settings

| Setting | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Dark Mode** | Toggling shows a brief confirmation message |
| **Booking Reminders** | Toggling shows a brief confirmation message |
| **Change Password** | Requires your current password first, as a security check |
| **Delete My Account** | Shows a clear explanation of the process first, with a **Continue** button that opens your email app with the deletion request pre-filled (or **Cancel** to back out). The practice confirms by email once your account and data are removed. |

### Logging Out

Available from **Home** *(icon beside your name)* or **Settings** *(bottom of the Support section)*. Either way, you'll be asked to confirm — tap **Log Out** to proceed, or **Cancel** to stay logged in. Once confirmed, you'll need to log in again to access your account.

## For Admin (Practice Staff)

### Viewing Bookings

- The **Admin Dashboard** shows all client bookings, a visual overview card, search, and filters.
- **The booking list updates automatically** — a new client booking, or a change made from the Booking Detail screen, appears here in real time with no need to refresh.
- Use the search box to find a booking by service, location, or notes.
- Search and filters *(Status / Location / Date)* work together.

### Managing a Booking

Tap any booking to open its details, then choose **Confirm**, **Decline**, **Mark as Paid**, or **Mark as Completed**.

### Viewing Analytics

Tap **"View Detailed Analytics"** for a breakdown of bookings and registrations by day, week, month, or year, including:

- Percentage change over each period
- A visual bar-chart comparison across periods
- A simple trend estimate for the coming week *(clearly labelled as a straightforward calculation, not AI)*

### Exporting Data

Tap **"Export CSV"** to save or share the currently-filtered list of bookings as a spreadsheet file.

### Updating Practice Information

From **Settings → Practice Information**, update the contact number, email, WhatsApp link, and operating hours. Changes apply across the app immediately.

### Logging Out

Tap the Logout icon in the **Admin Dashboard** header, or from **Settings**. Either way, you'll be asked to confirm before being signed out.

## Using the App on a Tablet

The app is fully usable on both phones and tablets:

- On a larger tablet screen, content is **centred with generous margins** rather than stretched edge-to-edge.
- Text is **sized larger** for comfortable reading at arm's length.
- All the same features and screens work identically — nothing is hidden or different on tablet, it's simply laid out more comfortably for the bigger screen.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely Cause | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Can't log in | Email not verified yet | Check your inbox for the verification email |
| "You're offline" message | No internet connection | Booking, login, and cancellation need a connection; other screens work from your last-loaded data |
| Booking rejected on a weekday | Date is a South African public holiday | Choose a different date |
| Can't find Logout | — | Home *(top-right icon)* or Settings *(bottom of Support section)* — either way you'll be asked to confirm before it actually logs you out |
| Forgot password | — | Tap **"Forgot Password?"** on Login |
| Pressed back and nothing happened | You're on Home or Admin Dashboard | Expected — press back again within 2 seconds to exit the app, or use a card/button to navigate instead |
| Got logged out unexpectedly | This shouldn't normally happen | You are only ever logged out by tapping Logout and confirming the dialog — if this happens any other way, please report it to the practice as a bug |
| App reopened and I had to log in again | Rare — should not normally happen | You should stay logged in between app opens; if you're consistently being asked to log in again, this may be worth reporting |
